# Security Policy

The Schwarz Digits Open Source community takes the security of our software
seriously. We appreciate the efforts of security researchers and the wider
community in helping us keep our projects safe.

## Scope

This security policy applies to all open-source projects published in the
following GitHub organizations:

* [SchwarzIT](https://github.com/SchwarzIT)
* [SchwarzDigits](https://github.com/SchwarzDigits)

For STACKIT-related open-source projects, please refer to the security policy
of the [stackitcloud](https://github.com/stackitcloud) organization, which is
maintained by the STACKIT security team separately.

For security concerns regarding Schwarz Digits commercial products or services
that are not open source, please use the official Schwarz Digits support
channels instead.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues,
discussions, or pull requests.**

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability in any of our
open-source projects, please report it to us privately by emailing
[opensource@digits.schwarz](mailto:opensource@digits.schwarz).

To help us triage and resolve the issue as quickly as possible, please include
as much of the following information as you can:

* The affected project and, if possible, the affected version or commit
* A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact
* Steps to reproduce the issue, including any required configuration
* Proof-of-concept code, screenshots, or logs (if applicable)
* Your name and affiliation, if you would like to be acknowledged

## Our Response

When you report a vulnerability, you can expect the following from us:

* We will work with you to validate and address the issue.
* We will keep you informed of our progress as we investigate and develop
  a fix.
* We will treat your report confidentially and will not share your details
  with third parties without your consent, except where required by law.

## Coordinated Disclosure

We follow a coordinated disclosure process:

1. After receiving your report, we will validate the vulnerability and assess
   its impact.
2. We will develop and test a fix, coordinating with you where helpful.
3. Once a fix is available, we will release it and publicly disclose the
   vulnerability, typically within **90 days** of the initial report.
4. We kindly ask reporters to refrain from public disclosure until a fix has
   been released or the 90-day period has elapsed, whichever comes first.

If you believe a vulnerability poses an immediate and severe risk that
warrants accelerated disclosure, please indicate this in your report so we
can prioritize accordingly.

## Acknowledgements

We are grateful to everyone who reports vulnerabilities responsibly. With
your permission, we will acknowledge your contribution in the release notes
of the affected project.
